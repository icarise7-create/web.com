Q: Should I take multivitamins in the morning or before bed?

Multivitamins should almost always be taken with breakfast or lunch. B-vitamins boost ATP metabolic conversion, which can impair sleep if taken at night. Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) also absorb drastically better with dietary fats from breakfast.

Q: Are the softgel capsules 100% Halal in Pakistan?

Yes, 100%. All softgels are encapsulated with certified bovine gelatin under SANHA Halal / Jamia Ashrafia audit, adhering strictly to DRAP regulations. No porcine gelatin is ever used.
